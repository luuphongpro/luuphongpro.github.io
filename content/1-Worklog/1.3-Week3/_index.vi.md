* Comprehensively understand Amazon EC2 and its instance types.
* Know how to use AMIs, create backups, and manage key pairs.

* Understand the Auto Scaling mechanism and the ability to automatically scale resources.

### Week 3 Outcomes:

* Clear understanding of EC2 concepts and components:
  * Instance Types and their use cases.
  * On-Demand, Reserved, and Spot Instances.

* Mastery of AMI:
  * Creating AMIs from a running EC2 instance.
  * Backup & recovery from an AMI.
  * Troubleshooting errors during launch from an AMI.

* Grasp of Key Pair management mechanism:
  * SSH key pair.
  * Restoring EC2 when the key pair is lost.

* Full understanding of EBS:
  * Volume types (gp2/gp3, io1/io2, st1, sc1).
  * Snapshot & restore.
  * Increasing volume capacity.

* Grasp of Auto Scaling:
  * Creating a Launch Template.
  * Configuring an Auto Scaling Group.
  * Understanding Scaling Policies (Target Tracking, Step Scaling, etc.).
  * Understanding the mechanism for automatic instance scaling in/out.

* Ready to combine EC2 + VPC to deploy a complete infrastructure model.
